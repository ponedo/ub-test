Attributes for TypeId ns3::UbSwitch
    --ns3::UbSwitch::FlowControl=[CBFC]
        Flow control mechanism (NONE, CBFC, CBFC_SHARED, PFC_FIXED, PFC_DYNAMIC, or PFC_DYNAMIC_PAPER). PFC_DYNAMIC_PAPER is the paper-style dynamic PFC mode used for DCQCN reproduction from "Congestion Control for Large-Scale RDMA Deployments" (SIGCOMM 2015). CBFC and PFC modes are peer policies over the same ingress accounting model.
    --ns3::UbSwitch::InPortProcessingDelay=[+0ps]
        Fixed non-blocking input-port processing latency after route/output are known and before the packet becomes visible in VOQ to the allocator.
    --ns3::UbSwitch::VlScheduler=[SP]
        VL inter-scheduling algorithm (SP or DWRR).
